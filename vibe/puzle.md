Here’s a categorized list of **puzzle game mechanic types**, covering a wide range of gameplay styles and logic systems:

---

### 🧠 **Logic-Based Mechanics**
- **Grid-based logic** (e.g., *Picross*, *Sudoku*)
- **Pathfinding puzzles** (e.g., *The Witness*, *Linelight*)
- **Pattern recognition** (e.g., *Zenge*, *Kami*)
- **Sequence solving** (e.g., *Simon Says*, *Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes*)
- **Rule deduction** (e.g., *Return of the Obra Dinn*, *Baba Is You*)

---

### 🔄 **Manipulation & Movement**
- **Tile sliding** (e.g., *Threes!*, *Unblock Me*)
- **Block pushing (Sokoban)** (e.g., *Stephen’s Sausage Roll*)
- **Rotation puzzles** (e.g., *Gorogoa*, *Monument Valley*)
- **Object alignment** (e.g., *Perspective puzzles*, *Echochrome*)

---

### 🧩 **Assembly & Construction**
- **Jigsaw puzzles**
- **Tangram-style puzzles**
- **Circuit/connection puzzles** (e.g., *SpaceChem*, *Human Resource Machine*)
- **Pipe/flow puzzles** (e.g., *Pipe Mania*, *Flow Free*)

---

### 🕹️ **Physics-Based Mechanics**
- **Gravity manipulation** (e.g., *Portal*, *Fez*)
- **Weight and balance** (e.g., *World of Goo*, *Crayon Physics Deluxe*)
- **Momentum puzzles** (e.g., *Portal 2*, *Infinifactory*)

---

### ⏱️ **Time & Sequence**
- **Time manipulation** (e.g., *Braid*, *The Gardens Between*)
- **Turn-based logic** (e.g., *Into the Breach*)
- **Real-time reaction puzzles** (e.g., *Tetris*, *Lumines*)

---

### 🧬 **Meta & Abstract Mechanics**
- **Perspective shifting** (e.g., *Antichamber*, *Superliminal*)
- **Rule rewriting** (e.g., *Baba Is You*)
- **Narrative puzzles** (e.g., *Her Story*, *Outer Wilds*)
- **Fourth-wall puzzles** (e.g., *There Is No Game*, *The Stanley Parable*)

---
